Crash tension inside a pinball you already know.

THE MECHANIC

Launch. Tick. Drain.

1 · LAUNCH
Tap the plunger. Ball drops into the top of the playfield with a small random horizontal nudge.
2 · TICK
Every peg adds +0.04×. Every pop-bumper adds +0.22×. The number in the middle grows in front of you.
3 · DRAIN
Five slots at the bottom: BUST · ×1 · ×10 · ×1 · BUST. Payout = bet × slot × multiplier. Bust means zero.
